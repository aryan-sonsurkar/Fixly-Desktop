# Fixly Demo — "The Night Before the DBMS Test" (5–7 min)

One realistic student scenario, start to finish. No feature tour.

## Prepare before recording (checklist)

- [ ] Fresh install of Fixly_1.0.10_x64-setup.exe (or clean test account)
- [ ] `samples/dbms-normalization-notes.pdf` in an easy spot (Downloads)
- [ ] Close unrelated apps/tabs; silence notifications
- [ ] Know your Supabase test login (signed in, dashboard visible at 0:30)
- [ ] Rehearse once — total must stay under 7:00

## Sample data

- `samples/dbms-normalization-notes.pdf` — one page of realistic
  normalization notes (deliberately a bit messy, like real student notes).
  Safe content, no personal data.
- No other documents in the account (so the flow is clean on camera).

## Exact questions to type (copy-paste during recording)

1. Workspace: "I have a DBMS test tomorrow on normalization and my notes
   are a mess. What should I focus on tonight and why?"
2. Document chat: "Explain 2NF vs 3NF using the Students table in my notes."
3. Action button: Flashcards (one click, flip one card on camera).
4. Planner: "Make me a 2-hour plan for tonight:Normalization only."

## What NOT to show

- Settings, diagnostics, model/about pages, updater banners, auth screens
  (be signed in before 0:00), other people's data, the console/terminal,
  long loading spinners (cut them in edit), pricing/beta/waitlist pages.

---

## Spoken script + screen/action sequence

### 0:00–0:30 — Hook: the actual student problem [FACE CAMERA or NOTES on screen]

> "It's 9pm. I have a DBMS test tomorrow on normalization, my notes are
> three pages of chaos, and I have no idea what actually matters. This is
> what I built Fixly for — let me show you what I'd do tonight."

### 0:30–1:15 — Open Fixly, starting situation [FIXLY dashboard]

Open Fixly (already signed in). Show the dashboard briefly.

> "This is Fixly — a study app with its own AI that runs on my laptop.
> Dashboard's empty tonight, no plan, test tomorrow. First, I'm getting
> my messy notes into it."

### 1:15–2:15 — Upload the real document [Documents → Upload]

Go to Documents → Upload → pick `dbms-normalization-notes.pdf` → wait
for Ready (cut the wait in edit).

> "That's my actual messy notes page. Fixly reads it — now it knows what
> my course covers, not generic textbook stuff."

### 2:15–3:15 — Ask AI Workspace a context-based question [AI Workspace]

New conversation, paste question 1.

> "I'm not asking Google — I'm asking about MY situation: test tomorrow,
> messy notes. Watch it tell me what to focus on and why."

Let the answer stream (cut long waits). Point at one concrete
prioritization in the answer.

### 3:15–4:15 — Document help: chat + action [Document detail]

Open the document → Chat tab → paste question 2 → show the grounded
answer. Switch to Actions → Flashcards → flip one card.

> "This answer comes from my notes, with the page attached. And one click
> turns the same notes into flashcards I can drill right now."

### 4:15–5:15 — Turn it into action [Planner]

Go to Planner, paste question 4, show the generated tonight-plan.

> "So now I don't just understand it — I have tonight mapped: what,
> in what order, for how long. That's the whole point."

### 5:15–6:00 — Persistence [close + reopen quickly]

Close the app, reopen, show the conversation and the plan still there.

> "Close it, reopen it — everything's still here tomorrow morning."

### 6:00–7:00 — The ask [FACE CAMERA]

> "If you're one of our testers: don't click every button. Take ONE real
> problem — an assignment, your own test notes, your messy week — and do
> it inside Fixly. Then tell me the blunt truth, especially if it wasn't
> useful. That's what this round is for."
