# Fixly v1.0.11 — Tester Mission

Don't test features. **Use Fixly for one real problem you have today** and
tell us honestly what happened.

## Pick ONE track

### A. The Assignment ("what do I do first?")
Upload the assignment brief, question paper, or related readings
(PDF or a photo/screenshot as PNG/JPG). Then ask the AI Workspace, in your
own words, what the assignment is really asking and what your first step
should be. Follow it for 15 minutes.

### B. The Exam ("help me actually learn this")
Upload your real class notes or a chapter you must learn (a sample,
dbms-normalization-notes.pdf, is included in samples/). Ask Fixly to
explain the hardest part simply, then ask it to quiz you on it. Answer
from memory before checking.

### C. The Chaos ("what should I work on right now?")
Tell the AI Workspace, plainly, everything on your plate this week —
subjects, deadlines, what you've finished, what scares you. Ask it what
to work on now and why. Then actually do the first item. If you have
several subjects with similar names, check whether Fixly asks you to
clarify instead of guessing — tell us if it guessed wrong.

## Report back (5 minutes, blunt is best)

1. What did you try to get done?
2. What did Fixly actually do for you?
3. Where did it misunderstand you, waste your time, or feel useless?
4. Would you open it again tomorrow for real work? Why / why not?

"I didn't find it useful" is a valid and valuable answer. We need the
truth, not politeness.
