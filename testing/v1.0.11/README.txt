Fixly v1.0.11 — Tester Install & Use
=====================================

1. INSTALL
   - If you received Fixly_1.0.11_x64-setup.exe in this folder, double-click
     it and follow the installer. (Otherwise use the download link from the
     tester email.)
   - Windows may show a SmartScreen warning ("Unknown publisher"). Click
     "More info" -> "Run anyway". This is expected for an independent
     student-built app.
   - The first launch takes a minute: Fixly starts its built-in local AI
     engine in the background. Just wait on the loading screen.

2. SIGN IN
   - Sign in with the account you registered for testing.
   - Everything runs on your own machine plus your Fixly cloud account;
     your documents stay in your account.

3. USE IT FOR ONE REAL PROBLEM (see MISSION.md)
   - Pick ONE genuine thing from your student life today: an assignment
     you don't know how to start, notes you must learn for a test, or a
     messy workload you can't prioritize.
   - Upload the real material (PDF, PNG, JPG) under Documents.
   - Ask the AI Workspace what to do first — in your own words.
   - Do NOT try every button. Use it like you would on a normal day.

4. IF SOMETHING BREAKS
   - Note what you were doing, what you expected, and what happened.
   - Screenshot anything weird. There is no wrong feedback — "I didn't
     find it useful" is a perfectly valid answer.

5. UNINSTALL (optional)
   - Settings -> Apps -> Fixly -> Uninstall, or run uninstall.exe from
     the install folder.

Requirements: Windows 10/11 x64, ~5 GB free disk (local AI engine),
internet for sign-in and sync.

What's new in 1.0.11 vs 1.0.10:
- Course-aware AI answers (Fixly knows which subject you're asking about).
- Reliable study-plan generation with one-click actions.
- Faster, safer local AI (no more frozen screens).
- Honest error messages with retry instead of silent failures.
