# Fixly v1.0.11 — Demo Script (2 minutes)

For classroom / poster presentations. Timings assume the local AI engine
is already warm (ask one question first, off-script).

## 0:00–0:20 — The problem (say it, don't show it)
"A student's academic life lives in ten places — PDFs here, assignments
there, deadlines somewhere else. Nobody tells them what to do first."

## 0:20–0:50 — Dashboard + Documents
1. Open Fixly. Show the dashboard: today's focus, upcoming deadlines.
2. Open Documents. Show an uploaded PDF going Pending → Ready.
3. Open it. Ask: "Summarize this chapter." Point at the page source
   citation under the answer.

## 0:50–1:20 — AI Workspace (course-aware)
1. Ask: "I have a DBMS test tomorrow. What should I study first?"
2. Point out the answer uses the student's own material and deadlines.
3. If time: ask about a second subject to show it switches context.

## 1:20–1:50 — Planner (structured actions)
1. Open Planner → Generate.
2. Show the strategy, the action cards, and the timeline.
3. Click one action ("Add Task") and show it become a real assignment.

## 1:50–2:00 — Close
"Offline-first, local AI, your data stays yours. Try it — scan the QR."

## If something goes wrong live
- Blank/stuck AI answer: wait 60 seconds (first load is slow), then Retry.
- Planner shows an error instead of cards: click Generate once more.
- Anything else: say so out loud — live debugging is also a demo of
  honest software. Then move on; do not wrestle the demo.
